Format Inspector Lite Cloud Privacy

Effective: 25 November 2026

Operator and contact

Operated by MIGYEONG KIM (김미경). Privacy and product inquiries: algorithmchoice@gmail.com.

Data handled by the hosted service

The service stores account email, hashed API keys, subscription identifiers, plan/status, monthly usage counts, abuse-prevention counters and webhook event identifiers. One-time API-key plaintext is retained only during the limited claim window and is removed after claim. Validation inputs are processed to answer the request and are not intentionally persisted by the application.

Purpose

Data is used to provide and secure the service, provision access, authenticate API calls, enforce quotas, prevent abuse, reconcile subscriptions and answer support requests.

Payment data

Card and wallet details are handled by Paddle as Merchant of Record and are not stored by Format Inspector Lite. Paddle separately processes transaction, billing, tax and buyer information under its own privacy terms.

Retention and security

Operational records are minimized and access is limited. Logs are bounded. Customers should avoid sending secrets or unnecessary personal information in validation payloads.

Your requests

Contact algorithmchoice@gmail.com for information handled directly by this service. Payment-data requests may also be directed to Paddle Buyer Support.
